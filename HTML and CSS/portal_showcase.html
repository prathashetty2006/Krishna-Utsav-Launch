"Krishna Janmashtami commemorates the advent of Lord Krishna, who epitomizes the virtues of righteousness, wisdom, compassion, and devotion. Our institution’s annual observance unites students, faculty, and staff through a curated series of cultural performances, competitive events, and traditional festivities."
